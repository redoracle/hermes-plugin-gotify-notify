# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome constructive contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Our goal is a technically rigorous, respectful community where contributors can ask questions, report defects, and review changes without intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other contributors with respect; critique code and ideas rather than people.
- Be specific and evidence-based when reviewing security, quality, or design changes.
- Respect privacy, disclose conflicts of interest, and avoid publishing sensitive information.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help make documentation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory comments, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without consent are not tolerated. Security reports must follow the private disclosure process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, request changes in behavior, restrict participation, or close contributions when necessary. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through a contact method on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/redoracle); do not disclose sensitive personal details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in confidence where reasonably possible, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, code review, and other project-maintained spaces.
